  <h2 class="paper-title" id="generated-iv-title">Generated Instruments with Validation Data</h2>
  <p class="paper-authors">with Dingli Wang</p>
  <section class="paper-abstract" aria-labelledby="generated-iv-abstract">
    <h3 id="generated-iv-abstract">Abstract</h3>
    <p>We reassess high-frequency evidence that policy-path repricing lowers inflation compensation. A frozen rate factor instruments FOMC press conferences. Our design separates instrument construction, external benchmark validation, and target deployment across three nonoverlapping samples. The factor is exceptionally strong and closely tracks the benchmark. Neither fact secures exclusion. Validation instead estimates the scale of generation error, decomposing an otherwise free invalidity bound into that scale and one structural correlation. This parameter indexes sharp sets, breakdown frontiers, and confidence sets robust to weak instruments and validation uncertainty. The negative response survives small contamination only. Strong prediction is not instrumental validity.</p>
